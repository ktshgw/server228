# SOMSAI: исходные запросы для оформления

Графика создана встроенным `image_gen` по согласованному макету меню.
Фон и логотип хранятся в ресурсах клиента и включаются в DLL; кнопки, цифры,
текст, карточки игроков и карт остаются настоящими элементами интерфейса.

## Фон

Файл: `client/enhanced-auth/osu.Game.Rulesets.EnhancedAuth/Resources/SomsAi/ocean-background.png`.

```text
Create a production-ready 16:9 1920x1080 background illustration derived from the attached SOMSAI menu mockup. Preserve exactly the lovely underwater painted game illustration style, the brown catfish mascot with whiskers and yellow/red/green Mexican sombrero near the upper-left outer edge, and the round golden pufferfish with cream belly near the upper-right outer edge. Preserve their design and expressions. Turquoise sunlit water with soft rays, distant submerged tournament arena, coral and seaweed clusters in bottom outer corners, a sandy seabed with shells and orange starfish. IMPORTANT: remove ALL interface elements, ALL text including SOMSAI, ALL panels, ALL buttons, ALL top and bottom toolbars, all avatars, all frames and dividers. This is ONLY the background art, not a screenshot. Keep a quiet uncluttered darker deep-teal central water area across the middle and lower-middle, suitable for overlaying real UI controls later. Keep the upper middle clean for a separately rendered logo. The fish should occupy only the outer top corners and should not cover the central 65% width. Fish entirely in frame. Seamless full-bleed illustration with no outer border. No letters, no logos, no watermark. High quality hand-painted turquoise and cream marine illustration matching the reference closely.
```
